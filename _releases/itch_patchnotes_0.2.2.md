🛠️ **Patch Notes - v0.2.2 (Hotfix & Polishing)**

Another hotfix update that focuses on balancing, some logic hiccups with our guests, and a hefty dose of "Quality of Life" for the UI and controls. Thanks to everyone for testing and reporting!

**Bugfixes & Improvements:**

**Guests & Daily Routine:** The very first guest arrival on day 1 is now strictly timed to 07:00-07:30 to prevent unnecessary idle time after opening the reception. Also, business guests will now *really* only spawn if you have physically built a superior room!

**Quality of Life (Time Control):** Your wish is our command! The time control (HUD) now features 4 buttons (1x, 5x, 10x) plus the pause button (spacebar). The pause state now remembers your last game speed and instantly jumps right back to it upon unpausing.

**Quality of Life (Menus & Sounds):** Monitor windows (like the guest list) now close logically one after the other when pressing ESC (LIFO). And if you are annoyed by the constant cash register ringing (cha-ching!) or the menu sounds, you can now specifically disable them in the audio options.

**Tutorials & Helpers:** The tutorial has received a fresh tip that reminds you about the cleaning and maintenance staff just in time when the first room gets dirty. In addition, the room status icons (e.g. for cleaning) now finally have dynamic tooltips that tell you exactly whether the service is just "requested" or already "urgently needed".

**Clean-up:** We removed some hardcoded texts, corrected the bar opening hours (12:00 to 23:30) and cleaned up some annoying engine code warnings in the background to make everything run even smoother.

Have fun building and managing your guests! 🏨✨
